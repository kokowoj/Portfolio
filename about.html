About Me

Hello! I'm Koko. Thanks for stopping by! ♡

I'm studying Creative Technology at Illinois State University, with a minor in Cognitive Science that emphasizes Computer Science. I'm also studying Game Design and Development at Joliet Junior College.

I love getting to work on both the creative and technical sides of a project. Some of my favorite things to explore are creative coding, game development, web design, and 3D modeling. I enjoy figuring out how things work and then finding ways to make them feel a little more personal.

Recently, I've been helping develop an information quality browser extension for an independent study, along with programming for a game studio capstone. I've also gained experience with IT support and managing projects with a team.
